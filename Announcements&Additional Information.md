## 5EPlay Professional Challenge Season 1
Basic Event Timeline & Information
- Open Registration Period: 00:00 September 7, 2026 – 24:00 September 11, 2026 (Beijing Time)
- Open Qualifier Dates: September 14 – 17, 2026 (Beijing Time)
- Group Stage Dates: September 19 – 22, 2026 (Beijing Time)
- Playoff Stage Dates: September 24 – 27, 2026 (Beijing Time)
- Event Organizer: 5EPlay
- Event Tier: Tier 2
Team Registration & Direct Invitation
This tournament features an open qualifier. Based on the global VRS rankings published in August 2026, the top 8 teams from the China Region will receive direct invitations to the Group Stage.
- Eligible Region: Teams registered under the China Region, defined in accordance with official Valve regulations and policies.
- Registration Qualification: Esports organizations holding a valid corporate business license or with an offline training facility.
- Registration Quota Cap: Maximum 24 teams for open registration.
- Invitation Issuance Date: August 10, 2026.
- Initial seeding for Group Stage will be determined by September 2026 VRS rankings.
Registration Process
1. Submit registration via Google Form (Link TBD)
2. Real-time registration roster updates will be posted here (Link TBD)
3. After form submission, send required organizational qualification documents to our official email.
4. All esports club qualification materials must be submitted within 48 hours after registration to secure your team’s tournament slot.
Open Qualifier
- Format: 4 groups with round-robin matches
- Group Composition: 6 teams per group
- Match Format: All matches are Best of 1 (BO1)
- Advancement Rule: Top 2 teams from each group advance to the Group Stage
Group Stage (Main Play-In Stage)
- Format: 16 teams compete under the Swiss System (mix of BO1 & BO3 matches)
- Advancement Rule: The top 8 teams after all Swiss rounds qualify for the Playoff Stage
Playoff Stage
- Format: Single-elimination bracket
  - All matches except the Grand Final: Best of 3 (BO3)
  - Grand Final: Best of 5 (BO5)
Prize Pool Distribution
Total Prize Pool: 70,000 CNY
- Champion: 25,000 CNY
- Runner-Up: 15,000 CNY
- 3rd–4th Place: 7,000 CNY each
- 5th–8th Place: 4,000 CNY each
Supplementary Payout Terms
All prize money will be transferred to the designated corporate bank account or team captain’s personal bank account within 1–3 months after the tournament concludes. All relevant taxes incurred from prize winnings shall be borne solely by the awarded teams.
Fairness & Eligibility Regulations
- The event organizer reserves the right to reject participation applications from any team or player with active VAC bans or a record of verified rule violations.
- The complete official rulebook will be released to all registered participants no less than two weeks prior to the tournament kickoff.
